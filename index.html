A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your questions,
thoughtfully answered.

What can I expect from my first consultation?+

We begin with a conversation about your health history, lifestyle, and wellness goals, including topics such as stress, sleep, and nutrition. This helps shape a personalized wellness coaching plan.

Do these services replace medical care?+

No. NaturaCare provides wellness coaching, lifestyle guidance, and natural support services. We do not diagnose, treat, or cure medical conditions. Continue consulting licensed medical professionals for your medical care.

Can you arrange a program for our organization?+

Yes. Our brochure offers on-site seminars, off-site retreats, quarterly coaching, and monthly wellness programs for LGUs and private companies. Call or email us to discuss your team, preferred format, and schedule.

How do I request a consultation?+

Call 0995 668 1437 or email naturacarewellnesscenter@gmail.com to inquire about availability and next steps. Appointments are arranged directly with NaturaCare.
